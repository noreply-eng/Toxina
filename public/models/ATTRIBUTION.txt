This 3D anatomical head, skull, mandible, teeth, cartilage, and facial/masticatory
muscle dataset is an adapted subset extracted and renamed for bilateral clinical
mapping from:

1. Z-Anatomy — The libre 3D atlas of anatomy (CC BY-SA 4.0)
   Authors: Gauthier Kervyn, Lluis Vinent, Marcin Zielinski, and contributors
   Source: https://github.com/Z-Anatomy/Models-of-human-anatomy
   Web-optimized Draco build reference: https://github.com/hpfrei/body-anatomy-3d-viewer

2. BodyParts3D — The Database Center for Life Science (DBCLS) (CC BY-SA 2.1 Japan)
   Original author: Kousaku Okubo
   Source: https://dbarchive.biosciencedbc.jp/en/bodyparts3d/download.html

Modifications performed by SONOTOX:
- Isolated craniofacial bones, teeth, nasal cartilages, epicranial aponeurosis,
  facial expression muscles, masticatory muscles, and upper cervical reference muscles.
- Centered scene origin at the craniofacial centroid.
- Normalized bilateral mesh names with patient-side anatomical suffixes (_L / _R).
